About this class

This beginner-friendly Bharatanatyam course introduces children to one of India's oldest classical dance forms. Students learn basic hand gestures (mudras), footwork (adavus), and facial expressions (abhinaya) in a structured, encouraging environment.

Classes are held in small batches to ensure personal attention. No prior dance experience is needed — just enthusiasm and comfortable clothing!

What your child will learn

  • ✅ Basic mudras (hand gestures) and their meanings
  • ✅ Fundamental adavus (footwork sequences)
  • ✅ Rhythm and beat awareness (tala)
  • ✅ Facial expressions and storytelling through dance
  • ✅ Stage confidence and performance skills
  • ✅ Cultural appreciation for classical Indian arts
